What this is

A State-Bound Asset (SBA) is a token whose transfers are gated by on-chain health checks. Here, an asset's market is checked against its oracle price, its pool's spread and depth, and its signer count. When a check fails, the contract halts the parts of the market that need that check to pass.

    What this page does not show

    • The oracle is a test feed. One admin key sets its price. It proves the wiring, not real market data. It is not a verified SEP-40 feed, and it should never be on a production allowlist.
    • The pool is ours. The Soroswap factory, router and pair were built from Soroswap's source and deployed by this project. They are not confirmed as the official testnet deployment.
    • Signers and the liquidator use local test keys. The demo signs with command-line keys. The Freighter wallet flow has not been tested in a browser.
    • Each demo run changes live state. It creates a market, locks a 1,000-unit test bond for a day, moves pool depth with swaps and liquidity, and changes the oracle price.
    • A failed run left market 1 behind. It is in Discovery with 0 signers, and its bond stays locked until its unlock time. Nothing uses it.
    • Degraded does not mean safe for lenders. A liquidator who seizes SBA while the market is Degraded cannot sell it until the market recovers. A long Degraded period can leave bad debt that no one clears.
    • Paused freezes liquidation. With a dead oracle there is no valid price, so the contract refuses liquidations. Bad debt can build during a Paused period.
    • Slashing is not implemented. The spec's fraud penalty needs a way to prove fraud, and nothing in the contract does that.
    • The early-withdrawal pause (Rule 09) is not implemented as specified. On Soroban, a call that reverts rolls back everything it wrote. So the contract can refuse an early withdrawal but cannot record a pause in the same call. Fixing this needs a separate, non-reverting function.
    • Interest is zero. Lenders earn nothing and borrowers pay nothing.
    • The allowlists are fixed at deploy. Oracles, adapters and settlers are set once. Changing any of them means redeploying NOVA.
    • No audit. Tests are 15 unit tests for NOVA and 9 integration tests against compiled WASM. They cover the paths we thought of, not a review by a third party.
